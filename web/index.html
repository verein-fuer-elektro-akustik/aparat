knobs to show on its panel; cables to other modules become its jacks

  • shift+a opens this menu at the pointer
  • drag a module by its title, neighbours make room, esc cancels
  • drag jacks to patch, pull a cable off a jack to move or remove it
  • ctrl-drag a jack for another cable
  • drag or wheel a knob, double-click to reset, right-click for midi learn
  • right-click a module: random, reset, duplicate, copy, remove, make composite, help, colour
  • double-click a composite's title to edit its inside
  • right-click empty space opens this menu
  • shift-click modules or shift-drag empty space to select several
  • delete removes the selected modules or cable
  • the keys a w s e d f… play notes, z and x change the octave
  • drag empty space or middle-drag to move the view
  • ctrl+wheel, pinch or + − 0 zoom the rack
  • ? opens the help
